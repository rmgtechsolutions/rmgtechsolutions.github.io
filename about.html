About us

One stop solution for every machine.

RMG Tech Solutions is a Delhi-based IT company supplying, installing and maintaining technology for factories, schools, offices and shops across Delhi NCR and Bhiwadi.

We started with hardware: fixing, upgrading and supplying laptops and desktops. Today we look after a business's complete IT, from the computers on every desk to the network that connects them and the cameras that secure the premises.

What we do

  • CCTV: camera planning, installation, recorder setup and mobile viewing.
  • Networking: structured cabling, switches, routers and Wi-Fi for offices, schools and factories.
  • Hardware: component-level support, upgrades and supply for laptops, desktops and servers.
  • AMC: annual maintenance contracts with scheduled visits and priority support.
  • Sales and purchase: new and quality-checked used systems, and buy-back of old ones.
  • Accessories: peripherals, storage, batteries, chargers and cables.

Who we work with

Manufacturing units, such as a factory in Bawana Industrial Area where we installed CCTV and manage the complete IT. Schools, for lab computers, networking and surveillance. Companies and offices, for bulk systems and AMC. And shops and small businesses that need dependable systems and honest advice on what to buy.

How we work

Every job starts with a site visit or a diagnosis and a written estimate. Work is tested before handover, with warranty terms written on every invoice. Businesses on AMC get scheduled visits and a single number to call.
