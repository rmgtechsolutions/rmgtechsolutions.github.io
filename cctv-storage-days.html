How many days of CCTV recording will your NVR keep?

RMG Tech SolutionsUpdated 2026

"How many days of recording do we have?" is the question nobody asks until they need footage from three weeks ago — and discover the recorder overwrote it after ten days. The answer depends on four things: the number of cameras, their resolution, the compression and how the recorder is set to record.

Sound familiar?

  • You needed footage from last month, and it was already gone?
  • Nobody knows how many days your recorder actually keeps?
  • The hard disk was replaced at installation and never checked since?

Know your recording days before you need them, not after.

How much one camera records in a day

Continuous recording, H.265, typical2MP camera ... about 1.5 Mbps ... ≈ 16 GB per day 4MP camera ... about 3 Mbps ..... ≈ 32 GB per day

These are typical figures for a normal scene. Busy views, rain, trees moving in the wind and night-time noise use more; quiet indoor rooms use less.

How to make recordings last longer

  • H.265 or H.265+ — roughly half the storage of older H.264 for the same picture.
  • Motion or smart-event recording — record continuously only where it matters (gates, cash counters) and on motion elsewhere.
  • A bigger disk, or a second one — most recorders have a spare bay.
  • Surveillance-grade hard disks — drives built to write 24×7, such as WD Purple or Seagate SkyHawk. Ordinary desktop disks fail early in recorders.
  • Export what matters — incidents, exam days or audits should be copied to a separate drive the same day.

Check it once, then every quarter

  1. On the recorder, open playback and find the oldest footage available. That date tells you your real recording days.
  2. Check the hard disk status — "Normal" is fine; errors or "unformatted" mean nothing is being saved.
  3. Confirm the recorder's date and time are correct.
  4. Make sure the recorder is on a UPS, so power cuts do not stop recording or damage the disk.

Common questions

How many days of recording does a 2TB hard disk give?

With 4 cameras at 2MP recording continuously in H.265, roughly four weeks. With 8 cameras at 4MP, only about a week. The table in this guide shows common combinations.

How can we keep CCTV recordings for longer?

Use H.265 or H.265+ compression, record on motion or smart events instead of continuously where suitable, add a larger surveillance-grade hard disk, and export important footage to a separate drive.
