Frequently asked questions

Should I buy a new or a used laptop?

A good used business laptop can be better than a new budget laptop at the same price, as long as its battery and SSD are healthy. Our guide on new vs used laptops explains the checks to run before paying.

Do you test used laptops before selling them?

Yes. We check battery health, SSD health, the display, keyboard, ports and charging on every used laptop before sale.

Do you supply laptops in bulk to schools and offices?

Yes. We supply schools and companies with laptops and desktops in bulk, with GST invoices.

Do you buy old laptops?

Yes. Send us the model and condition on WhatsApp and we will give you a price.
