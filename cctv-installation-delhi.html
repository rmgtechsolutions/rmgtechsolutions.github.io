Frequently asked questions

How many CCTV cameras do I need?

It depends on the entrances, the open areas and what you want to see clearly. We visit the site, walk through it with you and give a written plan with the exact number of cameras and where each one goes.

How many days of recording will I get?

That depends on the number of cameras, their resolution and the hard disk size. Typical setups keep between 15 and 30 days. We size the hard disk to match what you need.

Can I watch the cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up the mobile app on your phone during installation, so you can watch live and play back recordings from anywhere.

Do you install CCTV in industrial areas like Bawana and Bhiwadi?

Yes. We install and maintain CCTV for factories and warehouses in industrial areas across Delhi NCR and in Bhiwadi, and take up projects anywhere in India.

Do you give a GST invoice for CCTV installation?

Yes. RMG Tech Solutions is a GST registered and MSME Udyam registered business, and every job comes with a proper GST invoice.
