Before the CBSE board exams

Since the 2025 board exams, CBSE has made working CCTV with recording a condition for any school that wants to be a Class 10 or 12 examination centre, and exam footage has to be kept far longer than a recorder normally stores it. The weeks before February are the time to check, not the week of the first paper.

Our pre-board CCTV check covers:

  • Every exam-room camera: is it working, and does it see all the desks and the door?
  • The recorder: is it actually saving footage, checked by playing back yesterday's recording?
  • The hard disk: its health and how many days it really keeps
  • Date and time on every recording
  • A simple routine for exporting and storing each exam day's footage

The full checklist is in our guide: CCTV for schools: what CBSE board-exam centres need. Always read the latest CBSE circular for the current exam year.

Frequently asked questions

Do you maintain school computer labs under AMC?

Yes. One AMC can cover the computer lab, staff-room and office systems, CCTV and the campus network, with scheduled visits and breakdown support.

Can you check our existing CCTV before the board exams?

Yes. We check every exam-room camera, the recorder, the hard disk, the number of recording days and the time settings, then fix or add only what is needed and set up a routine for saving exam-day footage.

Our CCTV was installed by another company. Can you still help?

Yes. We check and maintain systems installed by others. If anything is still under another vendor's warranty or contract, we tell you first so you do not pay twice.

Do you supply computers in bulk with GST invoices?

Yes. We supply new and quality-checked refurbished desktops and laptops in bulk, with a GST invoice and warranty terms written on it.

Which areas do you cover?
