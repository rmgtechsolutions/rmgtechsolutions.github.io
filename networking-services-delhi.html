Frequently asked questions

Why is my office Wi-Fi slow in some rooms?

Usually a single router is covering too large an area, or walls are blocking the signal. Adding access points in the right places, connected by cable, fixes it. We test signal strength room by room before suggesting anything.

Can you set up the network for a new office?

Yes. We plan the cabling with you before the interiors are finished, install switches and Wi-Fi, and connect every computer and printer.

Do you set up networks for school computer labs?

Yes. We set up lab networks, staff room connections and campus Wi-Fi for schools, and can supply the computers too.

Do you give a GST invoice for networking work?

Yes. We are GST registered and MSME Udyam registered.
