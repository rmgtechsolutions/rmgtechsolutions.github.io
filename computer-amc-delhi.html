Computer and IT AMC in Delhi

One annual contract and one number to call for every computer, CCTV camera and network device you own.

An annual maintenance contract (AMC) with RMG Tech Solutions makes us your IT department. We visit on a schedule to clean and check every machine, respond first when something breaks, and keep a record of every device so you always know what you have. AMC can cover computers, laptops, servers, CCTV systems and your network together.

What's included

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance visits
  • Priority breakdown support with a single contact number
  • An asset register of every computer and device
  • Software updates, antivirus and backup checks
  • CCTV and network maintenance in the same contract
  • Clear advice before you replace or buy any equipment

What the yearly fee covers

  • Included: scheduled maintenance visits, breakdown visits and all labour.
  • Spare parts: billed separately at actual cost, and only after you approve them.
  • Why this way: you pay a lower fixed fee and never pay for parts you did not need. This is called a non-comprehensive AMC.

When something breaks

  • Calls and WhatsApp: answered the same day.
  • Remote help first: many problems are fixed over the phone or a remote session, without waiting for a visit.
  • On-site visits: the response time is agreed with you and written into your contract.

What you get on paper

  • A written scope listing every device the contract covers
  • An asset register of your computers, cameras and network devices
  • A GST invoice your accounts office can process

Who it's for

  • Factories and manufacturing units
  • Schools and institutions
  • Offices and companies
  • Shops and showrooms

Frequently asked questions

What does a computer AMC cover?

Our AMC covers scheduled maintenance visits, breakdown support and a record of every device. It can include computers, laptops, servers, printers on your network, CCTV and networking equipment. The exact scope is written into the contract before you sign.

How much does an AMC cost?

It depends on the number of devices and how often you want visits. We visit the site, count the equipment and give a written quote.

Are spare parts included in the AMC?

No. The AMC covers scheduled visits, breakdown visits and labour. Spare parts are billed separately at actual cost, only after you approve them. This is called a non-comprehensive AMC, and it keeps the yearly fee lower.

How quickly do you respond to a breakdown?

We answer calls and WhatsApp the same day and try remote help first. The on-site response time is agreed with you and written into the contract.

Do you offer AMC for schools?

Yes. We maintain computer labs, staff computers, campus networks and CCTV for schools. See IT and CCTV for schools.

Can one AMC cover CCTV and computers together?

Yes. Most of our business customers keep computers, CCTV and the network under one contract, so there is only one team to call.
